Sign2Connect is designed as an inclusive presentation platform, not just a sign translator. It enables presenters and audiences to interact seamlessly using sign and speech with live transcripts, slides, and accessibility-focused design.

The Q&A module is activated only after the presentation.
Audience members interact using voice, converted to text.
The sign presenter responds using sign language, which is converted back to speech.
This design avoids confusion and mirrors real-world conference interaction.
