How to Use Voice Beyond Sound

A simple step-by-step guide to get started

🌐 Step 1: Open Website

Open Voice Beyond Sound in your browser.
Click Login or Register.

🔐 Step 2: Login / Sign Up

Enter your email and password.
New users must register first.

🔁 Step 3: Choose Mode
  • ✋ Sign → Speak
  • 🎤 Speak → Sign
🧑 Step 4: User Details
  • Name & Gender
  • Input Language
  • Output Language
👥 Step 5: Interaction
  • One-to-One Mode
  • Online Meeting Mode
🎥 Step 6: Presentation
  • Upload PPT
  • Enable Camera / Mic
  • Live Transcript
